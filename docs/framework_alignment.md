## 1. NIST SP 800-61r2 (Computer Security Incident Handling Guide)

The Atlas SecOps response workflow strictly operationalizes the four phases of the NIST SP 800-61r2 lifecycle:

| NIST Phase | Atlas SEC-OPS Operational Implementation | Artifact / Code Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Preparation** | Pre-configured baseline images, hardened container definitions, Sigma/Falco detection rules, and pre-authorized RACI incident escalation matrices. | `docker-compose.yml`, `telemetry/rules/`, `docs/raci_matrix.md` |
| **2. Detection & Analysis** | Multi-signal correlation across EDR, SIEM, FIM, and NetFlow sensors within 35 minutes. Automated severity qualification (P1-P4). | `incident-response/cli/atlas_ir.py qualify`, `docs/main_courante_timeline.md` |
| **3. Containment, Eradication & Recovery** | - Containment: Non-destructive iptables network quarantine.<br>- Evidence: RFC 3227 volatile RAM/socket extraction & SHA-256 manifest.<br>- Eradication: Account revocation, SSH key purge, DB secret rotation.<br>- Recovery: Rebuilding from clean IaC baseline, Go/No-Go Checklist Q11. | `incident-response/cli/atlas_ir.py isolate`, `collect`, `revoke`, `verify` |
| **4. Post-Incident Activity** | Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys), Ishikawa cause-and-effect mapping, 14-60 day preventive engineering roadmap, tabletop exercise scheduling. | `incident-response/templates/incident_postmortem_atlas.md` |

## 2. GDPR / RGPD (Regulation EU 2016/679) Alignment

### Article 33: Notification of a Personal Data Breach to the Supervisory Authority (CNIL)
- **Trigger:** Potential breach involving 40,000 customer accounts with names, email addresses, delivery addresses, phone numbers, and bcrypt-hashed passwords.
- **Mandatory Deadline:** Within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach.
- **Implementation:** Automated timer and filing form generation in `incident-response/artifacts/notification_cnil_rgpd_art33.md`.

### Article 34: Communication of a Personal Data Breach to the Data Subject
- **Risk Assessment:** High risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons due to identity exposure and potential credential stuffing.
- **Implementation:** Direct customer notification draft in `incident-response/templates/customer_communication_template.md`.

## 3. European Union NIS2 Directive (EU 2022/2555) Reporting Milestones

For essential and important entities subject to NIS2, incident notification adheres to the multi-stage statutory timetable:

| Reporting Stage | Mandatory Deadline | Required Deliverable | Atlas Playbook Action |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Warning** | **T + 24 Hours** | Initial notification indicating whether incident is caused by unlawful or malicious acts. | Early warning notification dispatched to CSIRT/ANSSI. |
| **Incident Notification** | **T + 72 Hours** | Comprehensive update covering initial assessment, severity, and compromised assets. | Formal technical assessment filing generated by `atlas_ir.py report`. |
| **Intermediate Report** | Upon Request | Status update on containment progress and ongoing investigation. | Updated Main Courante timeline (`atlas_ir.py timeline`). |
| **Final Report** | **T + 1 Month** | Complete root cause analysis, severity assessment, and long-term mitigation measures. | Formal Post-Mortem deliverable (`incident_postmortem_atlas.md`). |
